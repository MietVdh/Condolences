About

This website is meant for clients of Begrafenissen Rummens in Tienen, Belgium. Using your personal condolences link, you can generate a printer-friendly list of all the messages you received. Simply enter your link, and choose whether you would like the date (and time) added to the messages.


In some cases, some of your replies to messages might get listed as if they were a message of their own. For this reason, you have the option of specifiying names of people whose messages should not be included. You can list the names of the people who replied to messages, to filter out their messages from the list.


When you click 'Submit', the list of messages will be generated. This might take a few moments. Once you are on the page with the messages, you can print them: simply choose 'print' in your browser menu (or Ctrl + P). You probably want to uncheck the 'headers and footers' option. You can then print on paper, or choose 'Print to PDF' as your printer, to save the messages in PDF format.
